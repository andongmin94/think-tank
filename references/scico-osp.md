# Science of Computer Programming — Original Software Publication

Choose `scico-osp` when the contribution **is the software itself**: an existing, useful, innovative tool in programming languages or software development. The Software Track publishes Original Software Publications (OSP); the paper is a short description of the tool, not a research article about it.

## Official sources — checked 2026-11-26

- [Guide for authors, including the Software Track](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/science-of-computer-programming/publish/guide-for-authors), read directly in a browser (automated fetches are refused).
- Official OSP templates: [Word](https://legacyfileshare.elsevier.com/promis_misc/scico-osp-template.docx), [LaTeX](https://legacyfileshare.elsevier.com/promis_misc/scico-osp-template.tex). Updates to a previously published tool use the separate *Software Update* article type and template.

## What the submission contains

- The tool, a description of typically **around six pages** in the SCP OSP template without changing its formatting, and documentary material such as a user manual. Test cases and design documents are welcome as supplements.
- The software must carry a recognised licence, such as an OSI-approved open-source licence. Select **Original Software Publication** as the article type in Editorial Manager.
- Regular SCP requirements also apply: a stand-alone abstract of at most 250 words, 1–7 keywords, 3–5 highlights of at most 85 characters each as a separate file, a title page with the corresponding author's email, a declaration of competing interests, and a declaration of generative AI use in manuscript preparation. Source files must be Word or LaTeX; a PDF is not an acceptable source file.
- Review is single-anonymized.

## Fees

Open access is optional; the subscription route has no stated mandatory charge, and published items become free to read after 48 months (open archive). Confirm the open access page if the authors consider paying.

## Prepare files

Fill the official OSP template from the evidence ledger and the inspected revision (repository, licence, version, documentation). Bundle the Markdown source as `manuscript`, the filled template as `editable_docx`, and its `conversion.json` as `conversion_record`. The software archive, manual and highlights file stay pending until they exist.
