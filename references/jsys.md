# Journal of Systems and Software — Research Paper, In Practice, or New Ideas and Trends

Choose `jsys` for software engineering work backed by evidence (empirical studies, simulation, proofs or other validation). The journal welcomes practical experience reports, replications and negative results. Its **In Practice** track carries industry-to-research knowledge transfer, such as applied research reports from real industrial settings; **New Ideas and Trends Papers** cover emerging areas.

## Official sources — checked 2026-11-26

- [Guide for authors](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/journal-of-systems-and-software/publish/guide-for-authors), read directly in a browser (automated fetches are refused). No hard word limit was stated in the parts read; recheck the track you select.

## Initial manuscript

- Abstract of at most 250 words that stands alone; 1–7 keywords; **highlights required** (3–5 bullets, at most 85 characters each, separate file).
- Word (single-column) or LaTeX (Elsevier template encouraged); a PDF is not an acceptable source file.
- Title page with the corresponding author's email; declaration of competing interests; **declaration of generative AI use** in manuscript preparation (a separate section stating the tool and purpose); research data deposited or explained.
- Review is single-anonymized. Authors may join the **JSS Open Science Initiative**: after acceptance, shared material (data, scripts, code, tools) is validated and acknowledged. Unshareable material must be stated explicitly.

## Fees

Open access is optional; the subscription route has no stated mandatory charge.

## Prepare files

Bundle the Markdown source as `manuscript`, the editable DOCX (or LaTeX source plus PDF) as `editable_docx`, and the `conversion.json` as `conversion_record`. Highlights, declarations and the Open Science package remain pending until supplied.
