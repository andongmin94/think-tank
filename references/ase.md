# Automated Software Engineering — Research Article

Choose `ase` for research on automating software engineering tasks, including tools built and evaluated from an open-source repository.

## Official sources — checked 2026-11-26

- [Submission guidelines](https://link.springer.com/journal/10515/submission-guidelines), read directly in a browser (automated fetches are redirected to a sign-in step).

## Initial manuscript

- **LaTeX is required** with the Springer Nature template (`\documentclass{sn-jnl}`); submit the source files, style files and figures together with the compiled PDF.
- Abstract of 150–250 words without undefined abbreviations or references; 4–6 keywords.
- A **Statements and Declarations** section (competing interests, funding and other applicable statements) is required; submissions without it are returned. Include a data availability statement. Document substantive LLM use in the Methods section or another suitable part; LLMs cannot be authors.
- Review is single-blind.

## Fees

The journal charges **no submission or publication fee**. Open access after acceptance is optional and paid.

## Prepare files

Compile the authored LaTeX with the official template, inspect every page, and record the build. Bundle the source as `manuscript` (plus `latex_source` when separate), the compiled PDF as `review_pdf`, and the `conversion.json` as `conversion_record`.
