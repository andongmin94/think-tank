# Information and Software Technology — Research Paper or Short Communication

Choose `ist` for research or experience that improves software development practice, including empirical studies of open-source projects. IST is a leading outlet for systematic literature reviews and mapping studies in software engineering.

## Official sources — checked 2026-11-26

- [Guide for authors](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/information-and-software-technology/publish/guide-for-authors), read directly in a browser (automated fetches are refused).

## Initial manuscript

- Article types: Research Papers (at most **15,000 words**; systematic reviews and mapping studies at most 20,000), Short Communications (at most 2,500 words) and Review Articles. References and appendices count, and each figure or table counts as 200 words.
- A **structured abstract is mandatory**, with Context, Objectives, Methods, Results and Conclusion; papers without one are not handled. 1–7 keywords; highlights encouraged (3–5 bullets, at most 85 characters).
- Word (single-column) or LaTeX; a PDF is not an acceptable source file. Title page with the corresponding author's email; declaration of competing interests; funding sources; **declaration of generative AI use** (tool and purpose).
- Review is single-anonymized.

## Fees

Open access is optional; the subscription route has no stated mandatory charge.

## Prepare files

Bundle the Markdown source as `manuscript`, the editable DOCX as `editable_docx`, and the `conversion.json` as `conversion_record`. Count words with the journal's rule (figures and tables at 200 words each) before export.
